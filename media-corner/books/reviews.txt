Title: Naming Nature: The Clash Between Instinct and Science
Author: Carol Kaesuk Yoon
Date finished: Febuary 5th, 2024
Stars: 4
Review: The first 10 chapters are wonderful, excellent, brilliant. I loved every second of them. Only once the book was winding down into the conclusion did I find myself presented with a false dilemma between human instinct and science. Her understanding of why we are losing our “Umwelt” places sole blame on science, but I would venture to say it is a major product of a capitalist, colonialist, and power obsessed society. She did not set up to come to this conclusion, but I feel as though it is the correct one. She even mentions the idea that the absurd amount of brands are clogging out Umwelt and becoming our new thing we order, but then doesn’t condemn the system that brings us such things (capitalism. She talks about how we can’t help but categorize people based on race, it’s part of the Umwelt, but that completely ignores the terrible and scarring history of white supremacy. I would love to read this again, however I will be stopping after chapter 10.
